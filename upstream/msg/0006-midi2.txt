ASoC: ice1712: claim the second MIDI port of the PHASE 88

The PHASE 88 rack has two MIDI interfaces, one on the front panel and
one on the rear, but its EEPROM advertises a single UART, so the driver
registers one rawmidi device and the rear connector stays dead:

  $ amidi -l
  IO  hw:0,0    TerraTec Phase88 MIDI

Terratec's own driver exposes both - "[PHASE88.Interface.Midi1]" and
"[PHASE88.Interface.Midi2]" in ews88wdm.inf, "PHASE88 MIDI Port 1/2",
whose changelog line for the second one reads "17-Sep-02 (Cl) - Mic28
Extensions (Midi 2)".  Both UARTs live inside the Envy24 (MMIO
0x0c/0x0d and 0x1c/0x1d), so nothing is missing except the bit the
EEPROM should have carried; set ICE1712_CFG_2xMPU401 in the same place
the board's other EEPROM errors are corrected.

Verified on a PHASE 88 rack: with a loopback cable in the rear connector
a SysEx frame written to the second UART came back on it byte-exactly
(three frames, no byte differences), and the first UART - the front
connector, which worked before - still works.  With both ports
registered and idle the card raised no interrupt on its IRQ line for
10 s, so an unconnected input does not put traffic on the shared
interrupt line.
