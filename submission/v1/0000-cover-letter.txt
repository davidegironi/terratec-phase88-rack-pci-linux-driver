Subject: [PATCH 0/6] ASoC: ice1712: support the TerraTec PHASE 88

The TerraTec PHASE 88 (PCI 1412:1712, subsystem 153b:1151) is identified
by the driver as an EWS88MT variant, but its front module uses different
hardware. The EWS88MT expander addresses NACK, so the driver fails to probe
the card. After correcting that, the EEPROM also advertises a consumer
AC'97 codec and only one MIDI UART, neither of which matches the board.

This series adds PHASE 88-specific handling while leaving the EWS88MT
path unchanged:

  1. Use the PHASE 88's write-only chip-select expander at 0x42.
  2. Guard consumer capture open when AC'97 initialization failed.
  3. Mark the PHASE 88 as having no consumer AC'97 codec.
  4. Initialize the expander's second register.
  5. Expose the measured Line/Mic switch for inputs 7/8.
  6. Enable the board's second MIDI UART.

Functional testing on two PHASE 88 racks was done with kernel 6.18.55,
and the driver was built against Debian's 6.12.111 kernel. The series
applies cleanly to the 6.18.58 source. Testing covered probe, playback,
capture, analog inputs, and MIDI loopback. The card registers as one
10-output/12-input multi-track device; the AC'97 timeout is avoided, and
the rear MIDI port passes a byte-exact SysEx loopback. The series has not
yet been built or functionally tested on 6.18.58.

Questions for review:

  * Is the EEPROM fixup in snd_ice1712_chip_init() the preferred place for
    correcting the PHASE 88's EEPROM flags, or should this board have its
    own card type?
  * Is the measured Line/Mic 7/8 control an appropriate interface, given
    that the other expander bits did not produce a measurable change?
